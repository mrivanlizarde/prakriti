# Prakriti

Prakriti is the content engine for Yoga Marga. It takes the teaching you already record, in the
studio and in the archive on the school's YouTube channel, and turns it into things people can find
and listen to: a podcast episode first, and later edited video, thumbnails, short clips, and a
library of each teacher's own words. It will do the work on its own, and nothing is published until
the teacher in it, or someone that teacher names, says yes. The first pieces are talks where one
teacher speaks alone; classes, where students speak, wait for Serenity's rule.

The name is the yoga-philosophy word for primordial matter, the raw material every form is made
from. It takes the raw recording and gives it forms people can find: an episode, a transcript, later
a video, each in the teacher's own words.

## How it will work, in five steps

1. **You record.** Teach in the studio as usual. OBS saves each recording to the shared Studio
   Recordings folder. Anything recorded elsewhere, such as on a phone, goes into the same folder
   (through Finder on a Mac, or the Files app on an iPhone). Older teaching comes from the channel's
   own export. After a one-time setup in the studio, the only routine step is a 10-second sound
   check before each session; until Prakriti can confirm it, play the test back yourself.
2. **Prakriti notices.** A small app on one Mac watches the folder. It waits until a recording is
   finished, then checks the sound before anything else, so a recording with no sound is flagged
   the same day instead of weeks later.
3. **A package is prepared.** On that Mac, Prakriti transcribes the recording, keeps the voices of
   teachers who have said yes, cuts any voice that is not clearly theirs, mutes the student names
   it finds, and makes the audio evenly loud. It drafts titles, show notes and chapters, lists the
   facts worth checking, and says in plain words whether the audio, video and message are ready to
   post. Before a piece reaches you, Ivan listens at the moments Prakriti is least sure about.
4. **You say yes.** You get one email for each review session, listing each piece with one link. One
   page shows the episode, the transcript, a privacy receipt and a short accuracy checklist. One
   button, "Approve for the Feed", approves it; "Not Yet..." sends Ivan a note instead. Approving puts
   the piece in the next release, and you can undo it until it goes live.
5. **It goes out, and it is checked.** Ivan uploads it at first, and later Prakriti does. A piece is
   called live only once the podcast feed or YouTube confirms it.

## What it will never do

- **Publish without a yes** from the teacher in it, or someone that teacher names, for that exact
  piece and its exact words.
- **Put a publish button inside a notification.** You always see what you approve.
- **Publish a student.** For now, a class goes public only with every student removed, and only
  after Serenity sets her rule, a lawyer has read a new class release the students have agreed to,
  and she approves that piece's privacy receipt. Whether a student who says yes in writing may ever
  be heard is Serenity's rule to set. Nothing from a session where students were present goes
  public, whether they spoke or only listened, until Serenity decides otherwise. Every recording so
  far has one shared microphone, so the first pieces are talks where one teacher speaks alone. Any
  voice not clearly Gabriel's or Serenity's is cut, and Ivan listens at the moments Prakriti is least
  sure about before a piece reaches the teacher who approves it. Only teachers who say yes in writing
  are recognized by voice; no student's voiceprint is ever kept.
- **Make an AI voice or face of anyone.** A teacher may make their own, in their own account. With
  that teacher's written yes, Prakriti prepares clean recordings for it and uses the result only for
  a script that teacher approved, always labeled as AI. Nothing of Serenity's without her written
  yes.
- **Write answers for a teacher.** The library answers only with a teacher's own words and the
  moment they said them.
- **Upload a raw recording.** Recordings stay where they are saved today, and no removed audio ever
  leaves the Mac. The heavy work happens there. Only finished, checked files go online, and the AI
  that drafts titles and notes sees teacher-only text with names replaced.
- **Delete an original on its own.** An original is deleted only when a person decides it, for
  example after a removal request, and a record is kept.
- **Open private coaching calls.** They are listed so nothing is lost, and never extracted, opened,
  transcribed or sent to any AI.
- **Reply to comments on its own.**
- **Fake footage or faces.** Nothing in a video is changed to show something that did not happen.
- **Make up a testimonial.** A student's words are used only with that student's written yes.

Prakriti never asks for a password. YouTube is connected once, by the channel's account holder,
through Google's own screen, with Ivan beside them. Prakriti's YouTube code has no way to delete a
video, and the connection can be withdrawn at any time in the Google Account, under third-party
access.
