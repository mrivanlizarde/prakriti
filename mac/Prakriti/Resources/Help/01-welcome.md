# What Prakriti does

:::unless video
Prakriti turns raw recordings of talks into finished work: a podcast episode, an episode ready for a
podcast host, a transcript with captions, and drafts of titles, show notes and chapters. It runs on
this Mac, keeps watching with its window closed, and stops before anything goes out, so a person
always makes the last call.
:::
:::requires video
Prakriti turns raw recordings of talks and classes into finished work: a podcast episode, an edited
video ready to post, captions, a transcript and drafts of titles and notes. It runs on this Mac, keeps
watching with its window closed, and stops before anything goes out, so a person always makes the last
call.
:::

## How it works, in five steps

1. **Record.** OBS, or any app that records, saves the session into a folder Prakriti watches. You
   can also put any recording into the Drop folder.
2. **Prakriti finds it and checks it.** It waits until the recording has finished saving, then checks
   its sound. A silent recording, or one with no sound at all, is marked Needs a decision the same day,
   and nothing is made from it.
3. **Prakriti makes the outputs you chose.** It writes the transcript, masters the sound into a podcast
   episode and writes a short report. If you turn them on, it also makes the podcast-ready episode and
   drafts titles, show notes and chapters. In Progress, in the sidebar, and the menu bar show the step
   it is on.
4. **You review.** Double-click the recording to open its page. Listen to the episode or the original,
   read the transcript, trim the start and end, cut what should not be heard, and make it again until it
   is right.
5. **You approve.** Prakriti copies the finished files to your Approved folder with a receipt. You
   upload them. Prakriti never posts or uploads anything on its own.

## What Prakriti never does

- It never changes or deletes an original recording, and never writes into a folder it watches.
- It never uploads, posts or sends anything.
- It never sends a recording marked as a class, or one whose transcript names Serenity, to a model
  outside your Mac. See [Models and API keys](09-models-and-api-keys.md).

:::requires people
- It never keeps the face or voice of someone you have not named and marked as having agreed.
:::

## Start here

1. [Capture the best video](02-capture-the-best-video.md): the light, sound and room choices that
   matter most.
2. [Set up OBS for Prakriti](03-set-up-obs-for-prakriti.md): the settings, once.
3. Turn on notifications so you know when a recording is waiting for your yes: choose Prakriti >
   Settings (Command-comma), click General, click Open Notification Settings, then turn on Allow
   notifications. [Show Me](prakriti://show/settings/general)
4. Record a 10-second test and play it back with headphones before the first session.

:::requires welcome
**Tip:** The welcome window opens once by itself. To see it again, choose Help > Welcome to Prakriti, or
click Show the Welcome Window Again in Settings, General. Its last panel can choose a folder to watch and
turn on notifications.
:::
