06Never

What Prakriti will never do.

These hold in every phase, whatever is switched on. Each comes with its reason.

  • Publish without a yes.

    Nothing becomes public without a named person’s yes, tied to exactly what will be published: the file and every word with it. Any change sends it back for review.

    A podcast episode cannot be fully recalled once apps have downloaded it.

  • Put a publish button inside a notification.

    Opening a notification shows the review screen. Approving happens there.

    Approving work you have not seen defeats the review.

  • Publish a student.

    For now, a class goes public only with every student removed, and only after Serenity sets her rule, a lawyer has read a new class release the students have agreed to, and she approves that piece’s privacy receipt. Whether a student who says yes in writing may ever be heard is Serenity’s rule to set.

    Students were told the recordings are for the private community.

  • Make an AI voice or face of anyone.

    A teacher may make their own, in their own account. With that teacher’s written yes, Prakriti prepares clean recordings for it and uses the result only for a script that teacher approved, always labeled as AI. Nothing of Serenity’s without her written yes.

    ElevenLabs lets each person clone only their own voice, and consent to a likeness has to be that person’s own, in writing.

  • Write answers for a teacher.

    The Library quotes what was said, with the recording and the second. It never writes an answer for a teacher.

    A quote can be checked against the recording. A written answer cannot.

  • Upload a raw recording.

    Prakriti adds no new copy of a full recording online. Only finished files go up, and only the teachers’ words, with names removed, go to the AI that drafts titles.

    Prakriti never uploads a raw recording or any removed audio.

  • Delete an original on its own.

    Originals are deleted only by a person’s decision: a removal request, or the keep-for rule Serenity sets.

    An original is the only full record, so deleting one is a person’s choice, with a note of who decided.

  • Open private coaching calls.

    Calls in the private playlist are never extracted, opened, transcribed or sent to any AI. A call found later is stopped and set aside.

    Those calls are private to the client.

  • Reply to comments on its own.

    Replies can be drafted. A person sends them.

    Anything outward needs a yes.

  • Fake footage or faces.

    No AI-made footage, and no altering a teacher’s face.

    What a viewer sees should be what was recorded.

  • Make up a testimonial.

    No invented or AI-made reviews, ever. Real ones only with that student’s own written yes.

    They would be untrue, and the law forbids them.
